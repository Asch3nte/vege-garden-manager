# Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026.

## In short

Pot'à Gérer is a gardening app **designed to respect your privacy by
default**. Your garden data stays **on your device**. No sign-up, no account,
no ads, no trackers.

## 1. No account, no identity

The app **never** asks you to create an account or to give an e-mail address,
a phone number or any identity. You can use it entirely anonymously.

## 2. Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter (gardens, zones, plantings, tasks, harvests, observations,
preferences) is saved **locally**, in a database private to the app, on your
device only. **None of it is sent to a server**, neither ours nor anyone
else's.

You can check what is stored at any time (Settings › Backup & data ›
"Stored data") and **erase everything** (Settings › Backup & data › Reset).

## 3. What may leave your device (and only that)

For the weather and place names, the app may contact two outside services that
are **free, with no account and no key**. This is the **only** case where any
information leaves your device, always kept to a minimum:

- **Open-Meteo** (weather forecasts, evapotranspiration, elevation): the app
  sends **your garden's coordinates, rounded to the kilometre**, to get the
  local weather and the elevation of the place (used to suggest climate and
  hardiness). Automatic weather is **on by default**; you can turn it off
  (point 5).
  [Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)
- **Nominatim / OpenStreetMap** (place names): when you place your garden on
  the map, the app sends its **coordinates, rounded to the kilometre**, to show
  the name of the town, only while automatic weather is on. If you **search
  for a place** by name, the **text you type** is sent to find its
  coordinates.
  [OpenStreetMap privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)

As with any Internet connection, these services see your connection's **IP
address**. The app sends them no identifier, no account and no garden data: it
only introduces itself by its name.

## 4. Sensitive data and permissions

- **Location.** It is only used to work out your climate and hardiness zone,
  and to get the local weather. You can set it **by hand on a map** (no GPS
  sensor used) instead of using geolocation. Either way, it is **rounded to
  the kilometre** and never shared beyond the exchanges described in point 3.
- **Notifications.** Reminders (watering, sowing, harvest, weather alerts) are
  **local notifications**, created and shown by your device. They go through
  no server.

## 5. You stay in control

Everything can be turned off, at any time, in the Settings:

- turn off **automatic weather** (Settings › Privacy &
  opt-outs): no more automatic
  exchange at all, neither weather nor place names;
- turn off **geolocation** or set the position by hand;
- turn off **notifications** (all at once or by category), set a "Do not
  disturb" window;
- **export** your data to a file only you handle, and **reset** the app.

## 6. Backups

Backups are **files created on your device**, when you choose. You decide
whether, when and with whom you share them. The app never sends them anywhere
on its own.

## 7. No trackers, no ads, no statistics

The app includes **no** audience measurement, behavioural tracking or
advertising tool. No usage statistics are collected.

## 8. Children

The app collects no personal data and is therefore suitable for everyone.

## 9. Free software

Pot'à Gérer is **open source** (MIT licence). Its code is public and
verifiable: anyone can check that these commitments are kept.

## 10. Changes

This policy may change along with the app. The applicable version is always
the one **embedded in the version you use**, readable offline in Settings ›
Privacy & opt-outs.

## 11. Contact

Questions, comments or reports: the app's "About" page, "Source code" link
(the project's GitHub repository).
